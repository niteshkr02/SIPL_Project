UPSTREAM & INTERMEDIATE

What Feeds the Furnace

Most of these never reach a customer directly. They're what makes everything downstream possible.

Iron Pellets

Spheroids of 6–16mm, mixing iron ore fines with limestone, dolomite and bentonite binders before thermal hardening, premium feedstock for DRI kilns and blast furnaces alike.

6–16mmSIPL Rukni0.9+ MTPA

Sponge Iron

Solid-state direct reduced metallic iron, made by removing oxygen from raw ore. India is the world's largest producer of this, and it's the primary feed for our induction furnace route.

Coal-Based DRIHigh Metallisation

Ferro Alloys

Silicomanganese, Ferromanganese, Ferrosilicon and Ferrochrome: iron alloys rich in manganese, silicon and chromium, made in-house for our own furnace and for stainless steel producers worldwide.

SiMnFeMnFeSiFeCr

M.S. Billets

High-grade semi-finished feed for rolling mills, produced by melting sponge iron, pig iron and select ferro alloys at ~1600°C, then shaped via Continuous Casting.

100mm–160mmIS 2830:2012

ALUMINIUM FOIL

The Newest Metal We Work In

After seven decades in iron and steel, Shakambhari rolled its first aluminium foil: 9 to 60 microns, made at SIPL Mogra, our India Foils facility in Hooghly, and exported to 29+ countries.

Pharmaceutical Foil

Bare and printed foil for blister packs, engineered to protect tablets and capsules from moisture, light and contamination.

9–60 MicronBIS Certified

Household & FMCG Foil

Everyday kitchen and food-service foil, plus semi-rigid container foil for ready-to-eat and takeaway packaging.

Food GradeISO 14001:2015

Lidding & Tagger Foils

Lidding foils with induction wads for sealed containers, and tagger foils used in bottle and jar closures.

Induction SealingISO 9001:2015

Rolled on an Achenbach Foil Rolling Mill, annealed on a Wesman furnace with controlled atmosphere, and coated on a Rotomec VMCH machine for multi-layer applications, backed by a six-stage quality protocol from raw aluminium to finished roll.
