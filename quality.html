QUALITY

Eight Checkpoints, Zero Shortcuts

Our motto is our quality statement: the scope of work starts at raw material procurement and doesn't end until the customer is satisfied. Every checkpoint below breaks down into its own workflow, click any step to see how it actually happens.

01020304 05060708
01
LAB TEST

Incoming Material Analysis

Before anything enters the plant, it has to earn its place there. Every incoming batch of iron ore, coal and dolomite is sampled and tested against a defined chemical benchmark, nothing moves to production until it clears that bar.

1. Sample Collection
A representative sample is drawn from every incoming batch, not just the top layer of a delivery.
2. Chemical Composition Testing
The sample is broken down and tested against the composition range each material is expected to meet.
3. Pass / Fail Evaluation
Results are checked against the accepted range before any material is cleared.
4. Accepted → Production
Cleared material moves straight into the production stockyard.
5. Rejected → Returned
Anything outside range is rejected and sent back to the supplier, no exceptions.
02
LIVE MONITORING

DRI Live Monitoring & Lab Analysis

Reduction is a live process, not a set-and-forget one. Temperature and feed rate are watched continuously while the kiln runs, and the sponge iron that comes out the other end still has to pass its own lab check before it counts as finished.

1. Feed Rate Control
Pellets and coal are fed in at a rate tracked continuously from the control room.
2. Live Temperature Tracking
Kiln temperature is monitored in real time throughout the reduction cycle.
3. Reduction in Progress
Oxygen is stripped from the ore as it moves through the kiln.
4. Sponge Iron Output
The reduced material exits the kiln as sponge iron.
5. DRI Lab Analysis
A final chemical analysis in the lab confirms the output meets specification.
03
LAB TEST

SMS Lab Analysis

Three different inputs converge before melting, sponge iron, pig iron and ferro alloys, and each has to be individually verified. All three are checked before anything is cleared into the furnace.

1. Sponge Iron Check
Composition of incoming sponge iron is verified.
2. Pig Iron Check
Composition of incoming pig iron is verified.
3. Ferro Alloy Check
Composition of incoming ferro alloys is verified.
4. Combined Clearance
All three inputs are cleared together before melting begins.
04
PROCESS CONTROL

Alloy Dosing & Lollypop Sampling

Melting is only half the job, what happens after determines the final composition. Once slag is removed, the crude steel is refined and dosed with alloys to a precise ratio, verified with a quick sample pulled straight from the melt.

1. Melting
Inputs are melted down into crude steel.
2. Slag Removal
Impurities that rise to the surface are removed.
3. Refining
The remaining steel is refined to tighten its composition.
4. Alloy Dosing
Alloys are added in a controlled ratio to hit the target grade.
5. Lollypop Sampling
A sample is pulled directly from the melt and tested before the heat is finalised.
05
LAB TEST

Billet Inspection & Documentation

What comes out of continuous casting gets checked from every angle, how it looks, how hot it is, and what it's made of, with every result logged before the billet is cleared for rolling.

1. Casting Output
Billets emerge from the continuous casting line.
2. Visual Inspection
Surface and shape are checked for defects.
3. Temperature Check
Billet temperature is verified against the expected range.
4. Final Chemical Analysis
A closing composition check confirms the billet matches its target grade.
5. Documentation
Every result is logged against that billet's own record.
06
LIVE MONITORING

Rolling Process Monitoring

Rolling runs under continuous temperature and pressure supervision, every billet is tracked in, the cooling water is watched throughout, and every bar is tracked back out, so nothing about the pass goes unrecorded.

1. Incoming Billet Temperature
Each billet's temperature is checked before it enters the mill.
2. Water Temperature & Pressure
Cooling water is monitored throughout the rolling pass.
3. Rolling Pass
The billet is progressively rolled down to its final section.
4. Outgoing Bar Temperature
Exit temperature is recorded as the finished bar leaves the mill.
5. Equalizing Temperature
A final equalizing temperature is logged to confirm consistent quenching.
07
LAB TEST

Finished Material Testing

Before anything ships, the finished product is tested across every property that matters, physical, mechanical and chemical, and the results are documented against that batch.

1. Physical Property Testing
Dimensions, weight and surface finish are checked against specification.
2. Mechanical Property Testing
Strength and ductility are tested to confirm the grade holds up.
3. Chemical Property Testing
A closing composition check confirms the finished material's chemistry.
4. Documentation & Clearance
Results are logged and the batch is cleared to ship.
08
DISPATCH

Lot Tagging & Dispatch Clearance

Nothing leaves without a traceable identity. Every lot is tagged, logged and cleared before dispatch, so any bar that leaves the plant can be traced straight back to the batch it came from.

1. Lot Identification
Every finished lot is assigned its own identifying record.
2. Physical Tagging
Tags are attached marking the lot for tracking.
3. Traceability Record
The lot's full production history is logged against its tag.
4. Dispatch Clearance
The lot is cleared and released for shipment.
