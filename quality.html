Quality

QUALITY

Eight Checkpoints, Zero Shortcuts

Our motto is our quality statement: the scope of work starts at raw material procurement and doesn't end until the customer is satisfied.

01

Chemical analysis of incoming Iron Ore, Coal & Dolomite. Only accepted material reaches production. Rejected material goes straight back to the supplier.

02

Heat temperature and feed rate tracked live by the control room; final chemical analysis of Sponge Iron carried out in the DRI lab.

03

Chemical analysis of Sponge Iron, Pig Iron, Ferro Alloys and other elements, carried out by the SMS lab.

04

After melting and slag removal, crude steel is refined and dosed with alloys to the required ratio, checked with "Lollypop" sampling for composition.

05

Visual inspection, temperature checks and final chemical analysis of the Billets, fully documented.

06

During rolling: incoming Billet temperature, water temperature and pressure checks; outgoing bar and equalizing temperature recorded.

07

Physical, mechanical and chemical properties of the finished material checked and documented before anything ships.

08

Lot identification and tagging marked. Material cleared for dispatch, traceable from that point on.

Between steps 06 and 07, our TEMPCORE quenching leaves every QST bar with a hard martensite outer rim around a softer ferrite-pearlite core: the exact microstructure that gives Elegant Steel its bend without breaking.
