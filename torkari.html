Small-Diameter Rebar for Ties, Stirrups & Lightweight Reinforcement

ABOUT TORKARI

Full TMT Engineering, Sized for the Job

A high-strength, high-bond deformed reinforcing bar used to strengthen concrete structures — engineered the same way as our full-size TMT bars, just built for smaller structural elements.

Fe 500 / Fe 550 Grade

Conforms to high-strength Fe 500 or Fe 550 (Tor-50/55) specifications, the same grade standard used across our full TMT range.

Tri-Ribbed Surface

Surface deformations on three sides for superior bonding strength with concrete, often exceeding 100% bond strength.

TMT-Processed

Made from low-carbon mild steel via cold rolling or Thermo-Mechanically Treated processing, the same discipline behind every bar we roll.

Weldable & Flexible

Highly weldable and easily bent into hooks or stirrups on-site without cracking.

MAIN USES

Where Torkari Goes to Work

Reinforcement: Acts as the internal spine in concrete members, slabs, columns and foundations, exactly like our full-size TMT bars.

Substitutions: Used at smaller diameters to replace conventional mild steel or lower-grade reinforcement in structural ties, stirrups and lightweight structures — the places where a full-size TMT bar would be overkill.

Being highly weldable and easily bent into hooks or stirrups on-site without cracking, it's the practical choice wherever a site needs to shape reinforcement by hand rather than order it pre-formed.
