SUBCATEGORY 2.1 · ISA EQUAL STRUCTURAL ANGLES

The Steel Behind Every Frame

Our structural steel range forms the skeleton of buildings, towers and fabricated frames — hot-rolled sections held to precise chemical composition, mechanical strength and visual finish benchmarks before they leave the mill.

ISA (Indian Standard Angle) equal angles are the core of this range: two legs of identical length rolled to a consistent thickness, used everywhere from tower legs and bracing to fabricated support frames.

SECTION TYPES

One Structural Range, Every Profile

Beyond ISA equal angles, our structural range covers the full family of hot-rolled sections used in fabrication and construction.

ISA Equal Angles

L-shaped sections with two legs of equal length and uniform thickness — the workhorse profile for bracing, frames and towers.

Channels

C-shaped sections offering strong resistance along one axis, widely used in framing and purlins.

Joists

I-shaped rolled sections sized for floor and roof support spans in load-bearing construction.

H-Beams

Wide-flange sections built for heavy axial load-bearing in columns and primary structural members.

I-Beams

Narrower-flange sections optimised for bending strength across long beam spans.

Chemical & Mechanical Testing

Every section batch-tested for chemical composition, mechanical strength and visual finish before dispatch.
