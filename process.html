OUR PROCESS

Everything Begins Underground

Long before a beam holds a roof or a bar reinforces a wall, there is only rock and fire. Shakambhari chose not to buy steel, but to build the entire chain that makes it — our own mines, our own reduction, our own furnace, our own hands at every stage. What follows is that journey, six stages from raw earth to reinforced tomorrow. #SteelWithin isn't a line on a label; it's the discipline running through all six, so that whatever carries our name carries the strength of everything that came before it.

THE FULL CHAIN

One Continuous Process

Every input, every merge, every transformation — mapped in a single unbroken chain. Keep scrolling: the chain moves with you, left to right, like material through a mill.

WATCHING THE CHAIN MOVE
CHAPTER I

The Earth Gives

Raw Materials — Iron Ore & Coal

Before a single beam is cast, there is only rock. Iron ore fines are sourced from trusted market suppliers; coal is drawn straight from Shakambhari's own mines — the first link in a chain we chose to own end to end, not rent piece by piece.

Iron Ore Fines · Market Purchase
Coal · Own Mines ⭐
ORE + COAL
CHAPTER II

Shaped for Purpose

Pelletization — SIPL Dhukuma, Bravo Plant

Raw ore fines alone are unruly — too fine, too inconsistent for the kiln ahead. At our Bravo Plant they're agglomerated into uniform spherical pellets, engineered for the permeability that clean, efficient reduction demands.

Bravo Plant
Spherical Pellets
PELLETIZER
CHAPTER III

The Stripping Away

Direct Reduced Iron — Rotary Kiln

At roughly 950°C, pellets and mined coal meet inside the rotary kiln. Oxygen is stripped away; only metallic iron remains. No coking, no blast furnace — impurities fall, and what's left is closer to the strength we're building toward.

~950°C
Non-Coking Coal
No Blast Furnace
ROTARY KILN
Strength isn't added.
It's revealed.

What remains after reduction becomes the core of everything Shakambhari builds — the #SteelWithin, before it has a name.

CHAPTER IV

Where It Becomes One

Steel Making — Electric Arc Furnace

Sponge iron meets externally sourced scrap and pig iron, and silicomanganese made entirely in-house — three sources, melted at roughly 1600°C into one liquid steel. Almost none of it was bought as a finished input we simply had to have; nearly all of it, we made ourselves.

~1600°C
Scrap & Pig Iron · External
Silicomanganese · In-House ⭐
ELECTRIC ARC FURNACE
CHAPTER V

Discipline Takes Form

Continuous Casting — Billets

Liquid steel is poured into water-cooled molds and drawn out continuously — no batch, no interruption. What emerges is a billet with a grain structure disciplined enough to hold whatever shape comes next.

Water-Cooled Molds
Homogeneous Structure
CASTING MACHINE
CHAPTER VI

Pressure Reveals Purpose

Rolling Mills — Finished Long Products

Billets are reheated and driven through rolling mills under enormous pressure, elongating and refining into three distinct products — each one built to hold something up: a wall, a roof, a skyline.

3 Finished Products
Pan-India Distribution

WHAT THIS BUILDS

From One Furnace, Three Futures

Every stage before this exists to arrive here — steel with #SteelWithin inside it, headed into the walls, roofs, and skylines of a growing nation.

TMT Bars

Thermomechanically treated reinforcement bars for RCC construction — marketed as Elegant Steel / QST TMT Bars. Somewhere right now, one of these is holding up a roof over someone's family.

Core Product

Narrow Strips (Hot Rolled)

Flat hot-rolled strips from SIPL Dhukuma, feeding pipe manufacturing directly or sold to external pipe mills — the raw material other builders build with.

Semi-Finished

Structural Steel

Angles, channels, joists, H-beams and I-beams, rolled to standard sizes — the skeleton inside the skylines rising across India.
