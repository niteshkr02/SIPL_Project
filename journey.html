ABOUT · OUR JOURNEY

Our Journey

From a single factory in Durgapur in 2000 to where Shakambhari stands today, and where it's headed next.

OUR HISTORY

Built One Decade at a Time

No single leap explains Shakambhari. It's a sequence of decisions, each one making the next one possible.

1981

SPS Steels Rolling Mills Incorporated

Incorporated on 26 December 1981, independent of Shakambhari at the time. Decades later, this plant would become part of the group.

1993

The Elegant Brand Is Born

SPS Steels Rolling Mills Limited launches Elegant as its flagship brand, more than 25 years before Shakambhari would come to own it.

1996

Formally Established as a Conglomerate

The commercial scaling benchmark begins: active investment in modernisation and structural integration across the group.

2000

A Small Factory in Durgapur

The group's operational story starts here: a modest industrial unit in Durgapur, West Bengal, with none of the scale that would follow.

1997 – 2003

BSIPL Incorporated, Then Commercial

BSIPL, today's SIPL Rukni, is incorporated as a private limited company in 1997 and begins commercial operations in 2003.

2010

SIPL Madandih Comes Under One Roof

On 13 December 2010, Shakambhari Ispat & Power Ltd takes over the Madandih unit, consolidating the group's integrated steel capability.

2012

Eloquent Steel Pvt. Ltd. Founded

Built specifically for ferro alloy production. The group starts making, not just buying, the alloys its own furnace depends on.

2014

Green Habit Is Born

Shakambhari Agrotech is incorporated on 21 July 2014, launching Green Habit: organic exotic fruits and vegetables grown in West Bengal.

2019

Elegant Joins the Group

Shakambhari officially acquires the SPS Steel Plant and takes control of the Elegant brand in April 2019, inheriting a name that had already been on TMT bars and structural steel since 1993.

2023

India Foils Joins the Group

Shakambhari Ispat & Power Limited wins the highest bid in an NCLT e-auction for Ess Dee Aluminium Limited, owner of India Foils. The Kolkata Bench approves the acquisition on 24 February 2023, bringing an aluminium foil plant into a group that had only ever worked in iron.

2025

One Company, One Name

In June 2025, the acquired assets are amalgamated and the merged entity is renamed Shakambhari Ispat and Power Limited.

Present

Multiple Sectors, One Umbrella

Multiple sectors, seven state-of-the-art units, under one umbrella: Shakambhari Ispat and Power Limited, part of Shakambhari Group. The diversification that started with a single Durgapur factory in 2000 is still moving.

2026 AND BEYOND

Still Building, Still Diversifying

A conglomerate is judged by what it adds next, not just what it already owns.

Where We Stand Today

Shakambhari is no longer a steel company that happens to make a few other things. It is five businesses under one standard: integrated steel across seven units in West Bengal, ferro alloys produced in-house, captive power that keeps the whole chain running, an aluminium foil division reaching 29+ countries, and an organic agriculture venture that proves the same discipline travels beyond metal. Every one of them exists for the reason the first factory did in 2000: to build something the nation can rely on.

Where We're Headed

Diversification here was never the endpoint. Ferro alloys in 2012, agriculture in 2014, aluminium foil in 2023, each was a deliberate step into a part of the economy Shakambhari believed it could serve better by owning rather than depending on. That instinct does not stop at 2026. The group keeps looking at where backward integration, in-house quality, and long-term ownership can be applied again, and whatever comes next will be judged by the same standard as everything that came before it: does it help build the nation forward.

Watch Earth Become Strength

Multiple stages, one continuous chain: raw ore from a mine becomes the reinforcement holding up buildings across the nation. This is where #SteelWithin begins.
