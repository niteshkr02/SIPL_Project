Heavy Structural Steel & Angles

An ISA (Indian Standard Angle) is a long piece of hot-rolled steel shaped like a perfect 90-degree letter "L" — a strong steel shelf bracket providing stiffness wherever two flat surfaces meet.

View Products Contact Sales
ABOUT THIS CATEGORY

The Corner Steel Every Frame Needs

Equal angles are used whenever a builder needs to join two walls, create a diagonal cross-brace, or build a rigid skeletal frame. Both legs are equal width, rolled to a consistent thickness from light window-grill sizes up to heavy transmission-tower gauges.

Rolled directly from pure steel billets, our angles feature crisp 90° corners with clean surfaces that allow for effortless arc welding and bolting without surface prep.
