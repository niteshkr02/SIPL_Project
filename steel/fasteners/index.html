ABOUT THIS CATEGORY

Small Parts, Real Discipline

Wire nails are cold-headed fasteners drawn from hard bright steel wire, used across construction and carpentry to join wood, formwork shuttering and wooden packaging crates, from light 1.5" trim nails up to 3.0" nails for structural wood framing. Low-quality nails bend when hit off-center; ours are drawn from high-rigidity HB wire so they drive straight without buckling.

Alongside them, pre-formed Link Easy stirrup rings lock vertical rebar cages square before concrete is poured, factory machine-bent, not hand-shaped on a dusty site.

WHAT IS IT?

Cold-Headed From Hard Bright Wire

Steel wire nails are cold-headed fasteners drawn from hard bright steel wire, featuring a flat checkered head that prevents hammer slips and a diamond point for fast driving.

PRIMARY PURPOSE & BEST USE CASES

One Nail Length Per Job

Used across construction and carpentry to join pieces of wood, formwork shuttering, and wooden packaging crates together.

1.5 Inch Nails (~38 MM)

Best for thin wood trim, fruit crates, plywood backing, and light furniture.

2.0 Inch Nails (~50 MM)

Best for general carpentry, door frames, and wooden scaffolding joints.

2.5 Inch Nails (~63 MM)

Best for medium-duty timber framing, wooden pallets, and concrete shuttering boxes.

3.0 Inch Nails (~75 MM)

Best for thick timber posts, heavy shipping crates, and structural wood framing.

WHAT IS IT?

Barrel Hoops for a Rebar Cage

When builders construct a concrete column, they stand several long vertical TMT bars together in a tall rectangle. To stop those vertical bars from spreading apart or bending under weight, they wrap steel rings horizontally around them, just like metal hoops holding the wooden staves of a barrel together.

PRIMARY PURPOSE & BEST USE CASES

Locking the Cage Before the Pour

Stirrup rings lock long vertical rebars in place so that columns and beams stay square and rigid when liquid concrete is poured over them.

On-Site Column & Beam Framing

Used by home builders and civil contractors to build skeleton cages for columns, ceiling beams, and foundation footings.

PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS

Why Choose Our Link Easy?

Bending steel rings by hand on a dusty construction site leads to uneven shapes and weak corners. Our factory-made rings are bent by computer-guided machinery for exact 90-degree corners, saving hours of manual labor on-site.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Link Easy, Common Questions

What shapes and hook angles are available?

Square, rectangular and circular rings, all formed with standard 135° seismic safety hooks for reliable anchorage.

Why choose pre-formed rings over hand-bending on-site?

Our rings are bent by computer-guided machinery for exact 90-degree corners, hand-bent rings on a dusty site tend to come out uneven with weaker corners, and cost hours of manual labor.

Can rings be supplied to a custom column dimension?

Yes, tell our sales team your column or beam cage dimensions and required ring size, and we will confirm availability and lead time.
