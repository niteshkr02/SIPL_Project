Concrete Reinforcement (Rebars & Accessories)

Concrete is like bone — strong under pressure, but brittle under twisting or pulling. Our reinforcement range is the muscle inside it: TMT/QST rebar that bends without breaking, locked into shape by pre-formed stirrup rings.

View Products Contact Sales
ABOUT THIS CATEGORY

The Steel That Holds Concrete Together

TMT (Thermo-Mechanically Treated) rebar is crafted through a rapid-cooling heat process that creates a rock-hard outer shell around a flexible, shock-absorbing soft core — placed inside liquid concrete before it sets so the finished structure can bend, not break, during earthquakes, heavy winds or traffic loads.

Alongside the bars themselves, pre-formed stirrup rings wrap horizontally around vertical rebar cages to keep columns and beams square and rigid before concrete is poured — the difference between a hand-bent site job and a precision factory-formed ring.
