FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions That Apply Across Our Steel Range

Product-specific FAQs live on each product page, these three come up regardless of what you're ordering.

What is the difference between Hot-Rolled (HR) and Cold-Rolled (CR) steel?

Hot-rolled steel is pressed at high temperatures (over 1,000°C), making it ductile and cost-effective for heavy structural beams and rebar. Cold-rolled steel is processed at room temperature after hot rolling, giving it precise millimeter tolerances, smoother surfaces, and higher yield strength, ideal for precision strips and tubes.

How does wire gauge (SWG) work?

Standard Wire Gauge (SWG) works in reverse: the smaller the gauge number, the thicker the wire! For example, a 4 SWG wire is nearly 6 MM thick, while a 23 SWG wire is less than 1 MM thick.

Do your structural products come with mill test certificates?

Yes! Every bulk shipment of our TMT rebars, structural angles, ESPL tubes, and wire rod stock comes complete with official Mill Test Certificates confirming chemical composition and mechanical strength tests.
