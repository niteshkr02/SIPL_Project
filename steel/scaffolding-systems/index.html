WHAT IS IT?

Temporary Steel Cages for Working at Height

Scaffolding pipes are thick-walled, heavy-duty steel tubes designed to build temporary metal walkways, towers, and staging platforms around modern construction sites so workers can operate safely at height.

PRIMARY PURPOSE & BEST USE CASES

Sized for the Load Above It

These pipes assemble using metal couplers to create rigid temporary cages that support workers, concrete mixers, and heavy building blocks during construction.

Light/Medium Staging (1.5" / 48 OD – 17 KG)

Best for exterior plastering, painting, and window installation staging.

Standard Construction (1.5" / 48 OD – 19 KG)

Best for multi-story bricklaying, concrete formwork shuttering, and masonry work.

Heavy Structural Propping (1.5" / 21–22 KG & 2" / 60 OD – 27 KG)

Best for supporting heavy concrete floor slab shuttering, bridge formwork, and heavy propping.

Square Frame Staging (2" / 50×50 MM SHS – 22–23 KG)

Best for rigid modular staging towers and base frames.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Staging Pipes, Common Questions

Which pipe should I use for heavy slab shuttering?

Heavy structural propping calls for 1.5" pipe at 21–22 KG or 2" (60 OD) pipe at 27 KG, both rated for supporting heavy concrete floor slab shuttering and bridge formwork.

What weight range do your scaffolding pipes cover?

17 KG, 19 KG, 21–22 KG, 22–23 KG and 27 KG per pipe, across 1.5" round, 2" round and 2" SHS sections.

Are these pipes load-tested?

Yes, every scaffolding pipe is tested against high axial loads to guarantee it will not buckle under dynamic jobsite conditions.
