FLAGSHIP · MADANDIH, PURULIA

SIPL Madandih

About SIPL Madandih

The Madandih plant, located in Purulia, West Bengal, is the primary integrated steel manufacturing complex of Shakambhari Ispat & Power Limited (SIPL) — the flagship company of the Shakambhari Group.

Operating on a "Metal to End-Product" integrated model, this unit processes raw iron ore and coal into finished TMT rebars, wire rods, and ferro alloys, while generating its own power from waste heat recovery.

What We Do
1
Primary Steelmaking

Direct reduction of iron ore in rotary kilns to manufacture Sponge Iron (DRI).

2
Secondary Steelmaking & Casting

Converts DRI and liquid iron in induction furnaces with Ladle Refining Furnaces (LRF) to cast prime MS and CRS Billets.

3
High-Speed Rolling

Operates continuous rolling mills to produce Thermocon HSD-QST TMT Bars and High-Carbon/Low-Carbon Wire Rods.

4
Ferro Alloy Generation

Smelts manganese and silicon ores for internal alloy consumption and external sales.

5
Captive Power Generation

Combines Waste Heat Recovery Boilers (WHRB) and Atmospheric Fluidized Bed Combustion (AFBC) boilers.

SIPL Rukni

About SIPL Rukni

The Rukni manufacturing facility was originally incorporated in 1997 and operated under Bravo Sponge Iron Private Limited (BSIPL). In July 2015, the facility was acquired by the Shakambhari Group under the leadership of Mr. Deepak Agarwal.

Following a strategic expansion and subsequent corporate amalgamation, the unit now functions directly as SIPL Rukni, an integrated production site within Shakambhari Ispat & Power Limited.

ALUMINIUM · MOGRA, HOOGHLY

SIPL Mogra — India Foils

About SIPL Mogra — India Foils

Located in Hoera, Mogra (Hooghly District), the Mogra facility was acquired and modernized by the Shakambhari Group to establish India Foils Limited.

Under the group's "Metal to Foil" initiative, India Foils manufactures precision-rolled ultra-thin aluminium foils ranging from 9 to 60 microns, serving global pharmaceutical, FMCG, food container, and flexible packaging markets across 29+ countries.
