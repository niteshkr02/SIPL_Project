ABOUT · GROUP COMPANIES

Group Companies

Integrated steel, ferro alloy, aluminium and agriculture businesses across West Bengal, each running under its own name and its own discipline.

THE GROUP COMPANIES

One Group, Many Plants

Every plant sits inside a single state, mapped and numbered. None of them cut corners to get there.

01 · FLAGSHIP · MADANDIH, PURULIA

SIPL Madandih

Incorporated in 2001 as Ma Chhinnamastika Steel & Power Pvt Ltd, renamed Shakambhari Ispat & Power Ltd in 2010. An integrated plant with an Iron Beneficiation Plant, Coal Beneficiation Plant, Rotary Kilns for DRI, an Induction Furnace with Continuous Casting, and Horizontal and Vertical Rolling Mills, backed by its own Captive Power Plant.

02 · PELLET & SPONGE IRON · MOHUDA, PURULIA

SIPL Rukni

Incorporated as Bravo Sponge Iron Pvt. Ltd. (BSIPL) in 1997, commercial since 2003, and associated with the group since 2004. Its Iron Pellets are produced at a scale that makes it a raw material source for other group plants, alongside its own Sponge Iron, Steel Melting and Captive Power facilities. A Hot Strip Mill and hollow and tubular pipe manufacturing are both underway.

03 · ALUMINIUM

SIPL Mogra — India Foils

Our unit in Hooghly district, and the one place in the group that doesn't work in iron. Rolls 9–60 micron aluminium foil, bare and printed pharmaceutical foil, household foil, semi-rigid container foil and lidding foils, certified ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and BIS, reaching customers in 29+ countries.

04 · INTEGRATED · DURGAPUR

SPS Steels · Unit I

Incorporated 26 December 1981, acquired by Shakambhari Group in 2019. An integrated plant in Durgapur rolling TMT bars, angles and channels, with its own Sponge Iron and MS Billet capacity feeding a DRI plant, Steel Melting Shop and automatic Rolling Mills.

05 · MEGA INTEGRATED · PORADIHA, PURULIA

SPS Steels · Unit II

The group's most recently acquired facility, in Poradiha village, Purulia, now being revived as a Mega Integrated Steel Plant: Sponge Iron through DRI Kilns, Ferro Alloys through a Submerged Arc Furnace, MS and CRS Billets through its Steel Melting Plant, QST Bars, and its own Captive Power Plant.

06 · FERRO ALLOYS · NACROJORIA

Eloquent Steel Private Limited

Producing high quality Ferro Alloys at Nacrojoria, West Bardhaman, for captive consumption and export markets, through a Submerged Arc Furnace and Induction Furnace. Built on two acquired plants, Hira Concast Ltd and Impex Steel Ltd, with Structural Steel next on the roadmap.

AGRO · GREEN HABIT · INCORPORATED 21 JULY 2014

Shakambhari Agrotech Private Limited

Under the Green Habit brand, SAPL grows a wide range of unadulterated organic fruits and vegetables, making fresh, natural nutrition accessible with every product. Beyond nutrition, it's become a source of employment for local farmers and the communities around them, proof that the group's discipline isn't confined to metal.
