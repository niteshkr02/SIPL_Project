ABOUT THIS RANGE

Every Wire Product Traces Back to One Coil

Hot-rolled, coiled round bar is where every wire product in our range begins — cooled and coiled straight from continuously cast billets. From there it's drawn, tempered or annealed into whichever product a downstream application actually needs.

That includes commercial-grade Torkari rod for light reinforcement, hard bright wire pulled through progressively smaller dies for mesh and fencing, and softened, annealed binding wire built specifically to tie rebar joints tight.

Commercial Wire Rods (Torkari Rods)

SUBCATEGORY 6.2

The same high-strength, high-bond deformed rebar engineering as our full-size TMT bars, drawn down to the smaller diameters ties and stirrups actually need — highly weldable and easily bent into hooks on-site without cracking.

Fe 500 / Fe 550 Grade

Conforms to high-strength Fe 500 or Fe 550 (Tor-50/55) specifications, the same grade standard used across our full TMT range.

Tri-Ribbed Surface

Surface deformations on three sides for superior bonding strength with concrete, often exceeding 100% bond strength.

TMT-Processed

Made from low-carbon mild steel via cold rolling or Thermo-Mechanically Treated processing.

Weldable & Flexible

Highly weldable and easily bent into hooks or stirrups on-site without cracking.
