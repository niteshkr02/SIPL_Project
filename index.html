Forged to Last

OUR PROCESS

From Raw Earth to Reinforced Tomorrow

Six stages, one continuous chain: our own mines, our own reduction, our own furnace, our own hands — nearly nothing bought as a finished input we could make ourselves.

CHAPTER I

The Earth Gives

Iron ore fines and coal begin the chain — coal drawn straight from Shakambhari's own mines, not rented piece by piece from the market.

VIEW STAGE →
CHAPTER II

Shaped for Purpose

At our Bravo Plant, raw fines are agglomerated into uniform spherical pellets, engineered for the permeability clean reduction demands.

VIEW STAGE →
~950°C · KILN

The Stripping Away

Inside the rotary kiln, oxygen is stripped from the pellets at ~950°C — no coking, no blast furnace, just metallic iron.

VIEW STAGE →
~1600°C · EAF

Where It Becomes One

Sponge iron, scrap, pig iron and in-house silicomanganese melt in the electric arc furnace into one liquid steel.

VIEW STAGE →
CONTINUOUS CASTING

Discipline Takes Form

Liquid steel is drawn continuously through water-cooled molds into billets, disciplined enough to hold whatever shape comes next.

VIEW STAGE →
ROLLING MILL

Pressure Reveals Purpose

Billets are reheated and rolled under pressure into TMT bars, wire rods and structural steel, each built to hold something up.

VIEW STAGE →
