SUBCATEGORY 4.1 · HR & CR SLIT STRIP

One Coil, Many Downstream Products

Flat-rolled strip produced by heating steel slabs and rolling them into continuous coils, then slit to the exact width a downstream process needs — from hot-rolled (HR) strip straight off the mill to cold-rolled (CR) strip drawn down further for tighter tolerances and a smoother finish.

This is feedstock steel: the base material our own tube division draws into ERW round, SHS and RHS sections, and a staple input for galvanizing lines, cold-rolling mills and general fabrication shops.

WHY IT WORKS DOWNSTREAM

Built for What Comes Next

Every coil is rolled with the next process in mind — whichever one that is.

Uniform Strength

Consistent mechanical properties across the coil, so every downstream part performs the same way.

Dimensional Precision

Slit to the exact width and gauge a downstream line calls for, minimising trim waste and rework.

Good Formability

Ductile enough to draw, roll-form or weld into tube, panels and structural sections without cracking.

Coil Feed, Not Cut Sheet

Supplied as continuous coil for direct line-feed into cold rolling, galvanizing or tube-welding processes.
