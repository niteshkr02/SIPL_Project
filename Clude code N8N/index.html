THE GROUP

Seven Units. One Standard.

Seven plants, one purpose: build strength the nation can rely on.

FLAGSHIP · MADANDIH

SIPL · Madandih Unit

177 acres, taken over in 2010. Houses the Sponge Iron Division, Steel Melt Shop, Rolling Mill, Captive Power Plant and Ferro Alloys division under one roof.

PELLET COMPLEX · RUKNI

SIPL · Rukni Unit

Commercial since 2003. The group's dedicated pellet complex, exceeding 0.9 MTPA: the first transformation in every chain that starts with raw ore.

ALUMINIUM · MOGRA

Unit Mogra: India Foils

The group's aluminium unit in Hooghly district. 9 to 60 micron foil for pharma and FMCG packaging, exported to 29+ countries under a "metal to foil" philosophy.

BRAND ORIGIN · DURGAPUR

SPS Steels Rolling Mills: I, II & III

Incorporated 1981. Three units that gave the group its Elegant brand, rolling TMT bars, angles and channels alongside Sponge Iron and MS Billets.

FERRO ALLOYS · 2012

Eloquent Steel Pvt. Ltd.

Built for ferro alloy production with submerged arc and induction furnaces, plus a metal recovery plant that minimises loss at every batch.

AGRO · GREEN HABIT

Shakambhari Agrotech

Incorporated 2014. Organic exotic fruits and vegetables under the Green Habit brand, among West Bengal's top organic farms.
